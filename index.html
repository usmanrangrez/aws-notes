How these notes work

  • Hands-on first. Every chapter is built around labs you can run in your own account, with cleanup steps.
  • Mistakes included. When something broke, the notes show the error, why it happened and the fix.
  • Production context. Each topic ends with how companies actually use it, plus interview questions.
  • Dated and sourced. Facts are checked against the official docs and marked with the date they were checked.
